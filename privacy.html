Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

NovaDock is a free desktop app for Windows. We collect as little as possible, and this page lists all of it.

Your email (download sign-up)

To get NovaDock you enter your email on this website. We use it to email you the download link, and occasionally to tell you about important updates. It is stored in a private Google Sheet that only we can see, and the email is sent from our Gmail account. We never sell or share it. To be removed, reply to any of our emails or write to us.

Anonymous usage stats (in the app)

To learn whether people keep using NovaDock, the app sends a small record about every 15 minutes: how many minutes and days it has been used, how many times it was started, its version, the Windows version, the theme and how many widgets are on the dock and menu bar. It is stored under a random ID made from a one-way hash of your PC's Windows ID, so we cannot tell who you are. It never includes your name, email, files, windows, what you type or your widget data. You can turn it off in Settings → Feedback → Share anonymous usage stats. The records are kept in Google Firebase.

Feedback you send

If you use Settings → Feedback, we receive your star rating, your message and, only if you type it, your email, so we can reply.

What stays on your PC

  • No account is required.
  • Your settings, notes, to-dos, calendar links and API keys are stored only on your PC, in your Windows AppData folder.
  • Widgets you turn on — weather (Open-Meteo), calendar feeds you add, AI providers with your own key, custom API widgets — connect directly from your PC to those services. We never see this data.

This website

This website does not use cookies. It counts visits and button clicks with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly service that stores no personal data and sets no cookies. It loads fonts from Google Fonts. The playground runs entirely in your browser with sample data.

Your rights

You can ask us to delete your email or any feedback you sent at any time by emailing us.
