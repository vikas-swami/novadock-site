Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

NovaDock is a desktop app for Windows. We built it to work without collecting your personal data.

What the app does not collect

  • No account or sign-up is required.
  • No analytics, telemetry or tracking of any kind.
  • Your settings, notes, to-dos, calendar links and API keys are stored only on your PC, in your Windows AppData folder.

When the app connects to the internet

  • License activation (Pro): your license key is sent to our payment provider, Dodo Payments, to check that it is valid. This happens on activation and about once a week.
  • Widgets you turn on: weather (Open-Meteo), calendar feeds you add, and custom API widgets connect directly from your PC to those services. We never see this data.

Purchases

Payments are processed by Dodo Payments, which acts as the Merchant of Record. Dodo collects the information needed to process your payment (such as your name, email, country and payment details) under its own privacy policy. We receive your email address and order details so we can provide your license and support.

This website

This website does not use cookies or analytics. It loads fonts from Google Fonts. The playground runs entirely in your browser with sample data.

Your rights

You can ask us to delete the order information we hold about you at any time by emailing us.
