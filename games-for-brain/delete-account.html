Delete your Games for Brain account

Games for Brain is published on Google Play by Arixverse.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to vikasmc100@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject Delete my Games for Brain account.
  3. Send it from the Google account you signed in with, or write that Google account's email address in the message, so we can find your account.
  4. We confirm by email and delete your account within 30 days.
Email a deletion request

What is deleted

  • Your account and its user ID
  • Your name and email address from your Google sign-in
  • Any game progress linked to your account

What is kept

  • We keep nothing linked to your account after deletion. Only the email exchange about your request is kept, for up to 90 days, as a record that we handled it.
  • Ads in the game are served by Google AdMob. AdMob processes device identifiers and diagnostics under Google's Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads.

Delete only your progress

Uninstalling the app removes the progress saved on your phone. To remove only the data linked to your account without closing the account, follow the steps above and say so in your email.
