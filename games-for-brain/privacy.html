Privacy Policy

Games for Brain · published on Google Play by Arixverse · Last updated 17 November 2026

This policy explains what information Games for Brain ("the game") collects, why, and the choices you have. Contact: vikasmc100@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Google sign-in (optional): if you sign in, we receive your name, email address and a user ID from Google, stored in Google Firebase, to keep your account and progress.
  • Game data: your scores, daily-challenge results, a game-made player name (for example "Swift Tiger") and a friend code, used for leaderboards and friends. Leaderboards show only the game-made name and score, never your real name or email.
  • Birth year: the game asks for your year of birth to give children an age-appropriate experience. It is kept on your device.
  • Ads: the game shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location from your IP address, app interactions and diagnostics to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. For children, ads are requested as child-directed and are not personalized.
  • Purchases: the optional "Remove Ads" purchase is handled by Google Play. We don't receive your payment details.
  • Crash and diagnostics data to help us fix problems.

How we use it

To run the game and its features (accounts, saved progress, leaderboards, friends), to show ads that keep the game free, to fix bugs, and to prevent fraud. We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing

We share data only with the services that run the game: Google Firebase (accounts and game data), Google AdMob (ads) and Google Play (downloads and purchases). Their privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and follows the Google Play Families Policy. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without a parent's consent; children should play without signing in, or a parent should handle sign-in. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Security and retention

Data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. We keep account and game data while your account exists and delete it within 30 days of a deletion request.

Your choices

  • Play without signing in.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Delete your account and data: how to request deletion.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date at the top when we do.
